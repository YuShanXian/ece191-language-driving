Historical MTR prototype guide

The former candidate viewer accepted limited English commands, displayed
pretrained candidates and explained missing or rejected choices. Companion
pages showed video/GPS alignment, observed action-stage replay and reports.
A model score was a relative candidate score, not a calibrated safety score.
Recorded future trajectories were comparison evidence and did not drive selection.

Map-tail and reserve-tail experiments generated separately labeled geometric
adjustments while retaining original outputs. Geometry, body orientation,
margin and modeled tracking were separate checks. A green historical route did
not imply it passed later diagnostics. Observed-stage replay followed the real
recorded driver; it did not mean the selected candidate had been executed.

Data, weights, third-party source, converted inputs, configurations and results
were separate resources. Tagged runs checked input/output fingerprints and
refused conflicting reuse. Reproducing cached local experiments did not validate
a new installation or a complete research pipeline.

The guide described conditional video/GPS alignment and sampled original-camera
checks, limited grammar coverage, sparse turning candidates, partial maneuvers,
and controller/traffic limitations. All associated output trees remain external.
For the current application, follow sim/README.md instead of these old commands.
