Historical offline MTR research summary

The earlier workflow converted nuPlan scenes to pretrained MTR inputs, used
controlled-language rules to select candidates, and separately inspected road
geometry, vehicle footprint, tracking and current traffic context. It did not
train MTR or implement arbitrary language-conditioned driving.

The fixed baseline covered 18 windows from six records; a separate turning
challenge used 13 windows from four additional records. Of 832 challenge
candidates, 62 passed the frozen checks. A left-turn suggestion existed at one
window; none existed for right turns. The accepted left turn was partial.

Geometry did not guarantee trackability. Four of nine lane-change routes kept
the target margin under all six tested settings. The optional tracking filter
reduced available selections from six to two of 30 active direction/time cases.
The LQR/iLQR comparison had mixed errors and no real-time guarantee. A separate
terminal-hold experiment extended the saved prefix without new control updates.
Traffic context and stop-region contact were descriptive, not legal decisions.

Historical verification reported 122 functional tests after five stop-region
tests were added. The earlier unified receipt covered 29 steps and 117 tests;
the later 31-step entry point was not claimed complete in this note. These are
historical claims, not results from this repository's current test run.

Outstanding work included independent alignment evidence, richer maneuvers,
traffic interaction, full replanning, fresh installation and real-vehicle
validation. No historical usage-management action is requested by this archive.
