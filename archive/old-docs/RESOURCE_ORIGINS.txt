Resource origins

nuPlan databases provide recorded vehicle and surrounding-actor state; nuPlan
maps provide lane and connection geometry. doPlan annotations and associated
video/GPS resources support human interpretation and temporal association.
The UC Merced share is storage for viewing resources, not a model or a replacement
for the raw nuPlan database/map package. Private sharing details are not included.

Public source links recorded in the original note:
https://github.com/Mi3-Lab/doPlan
https://arxiv.org/html/2609.38028v1

The annotation-window start is not a known passenger utterance time. Counts in
a downloaded annotation release may differ from a paper version; report the
actual release and its fingerprint rather than mixing populations.
See ../../docs/DATA.md for current map placement and dataset links.
