Stop-region geometry is not a legal crossing decision

The diagnostic distinguished center-point, front-edge and full-body contact
with mapped stop regions. It retained explicit map associations and compared
with the installed SDK's traffic-light stop-region selection.

Among 62 previously passing candidates, the audit recorded 39 raw associations
and ten SDK traffic-light stop-region selections. Center/front/body contact
counts were 4/4/11. The three candidates linked to a current red signal already
had front-edge overlap at the start, so a later center entry was not the first
front crossing. A partial left turn contacted a TURN_STOP region without known
current light state.

Contact does not establish a legal violation, stopping obligation or permission
to proceed. Current signal state is not arrival-time state, and sampled absence
of contact does not exclude between-sample contact. Evidence:
runs/project-v2/stop-region-geometry-v1/ and config/stop-region-geometry-v1.json.
