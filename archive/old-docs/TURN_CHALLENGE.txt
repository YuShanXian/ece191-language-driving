Candidate coverage in additional turning contexts

Four additional nuPlan records were selected under fixed size and deduplication
rules. Fixed relative times plus a qualifying official turn event yielded 13
windows. All 64 internal candidates and the original five-index final subset
were checked under the same frozen rules.

Of 832 candidates, 62 passed. A left-turn suggestion existed in one window;
right-turn suggestions existed in none. Both candidate pools gave the same
window coverage in this population. The selected left turn only partially
rotated through a connector and did not establish full maneuver completion.

Subsequent LQR tracking of that selected route failed road checks in all six
settings, despite passing the constant-velocity surrounding-car check. The
record retained these failures rather than rewriting historical coverage.
This was limited modeled evidence, not an observed accident or a balanced
language benchmark. Evidence: runs/project-v2/turn-challenge-v1/ and
runs/project-v2/turn-tracking-v1/.
