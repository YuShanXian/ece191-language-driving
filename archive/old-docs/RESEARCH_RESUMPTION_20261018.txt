Historical turn-attribution checkpoint

An earlier project continuation began a full local verification and added
independent attribution of turning failures. The note explicitly required
reading the final verification receipt rather than assuming completion.

For 13 windows and 832 candidates, 636 derived routes could not be fully matched
by the existing road matcher; their action was unresolved, not conclusively
proved off-road or turn-free. Left-turn attribution found 185 matched routes
without the requested turn, four with incompatible combined actions, six
rejected action matches and one passing match. Right-turn attribution found
195 nonmatching routes, one rejected match and none passing.

All seven rejected action-matching turns had constant-velocity collision flags;
three also failed body-on-road checks. These were forecast-model flags, not
verified actual collisions. Recorded futures were used for offline diagnostics
only. The sample was not a balanced left/right-turn benchmark. No continuation
or usage-management action is requested by preserving this note.
