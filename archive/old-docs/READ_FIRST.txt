Historical setup checkpoint

The initial work verified a WSL Python/PyTorch/CUDA environment, native MTR
operators, a pretrained checkpoint and inference on an original-format scene.
That established a motion-forecasting setup, not language-conditioned planning.
The next phases converted nuPlan inputs, aligned doPlan references, and explored
language selection plus road and motion checks.

The upstream source was Valeo4Cast/UniTraj MTR; local compatibility changes were
recorded in upstream-compatibility.patch. The historical doPlan checkout was
incomplete because of platform-incompatible filenames; selected public files
were obtained separately. Local file hashes establish identity but cannot by
themselves establish publisher provenance. Private discussion and sharing details
are omitted. The historical GPU/environment claims have not been revalidated as
part of publishing this repository.
