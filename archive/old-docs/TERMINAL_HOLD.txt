initializer required enough future reference points. The terminal experiment
preserved every earlier value in all 60 runs and appended four motion steps to
reach the existing six-second reference horizon.

calling the controller again or applying extra filtering. The last update
remained at 5.6 seconds. The full trajectory and surrounding-car time base were
rechecked, with no additional road or constant-velocity overlap failures.
This was a terminal-hold variant, not a repaired SDK initializer, six seconds of
normal periodic feedback, a complete turn or traffic-rule validation.
