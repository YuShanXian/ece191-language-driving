Following a fixed route with a vehicle model

Nine accepted lane-change routes were tested with the installed nuPlan LQR and
kinematic bicycle model. Three initial tire-angle assumptions and two motion
steps produced 54 trials. Initial state came from current/past observations;
the target was a saved candidate rather than the recorded future. Body-center
references were converted to rear-axle references under the stated assumption.

35/54 trials retained the final target-lane margin; four of nine routes retained
it in all six settings. The margin and initial-angle range were diagnostic
assumptions, not calibrated real-world safety tolerances. Candidate 41 in the
second annotation lost the target margin in every tested setting.

The results describe one fixed six-second route, not repeated replanning,
reactive traffic or real-vehicle control. No historical selection was silently
changed. Evidence: runs/project-v2/tracking-diagnostic-v1/ and the related
configuration. Finer steps also changed feedback frequency in this experiment.
