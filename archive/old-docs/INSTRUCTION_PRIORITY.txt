Instruction-first development experiment

The seven scenes and 35 manually reviewed instructions were development data.
Original reviews were retained. Action matching was prioritized while road,
collision and motion concerns remained explicit risk annotations. Lane changes
required a connected adjacent-road target and sustained occupancy; lateral
motion alone did not establish a lane change. Turns were local geometric goals,
not full intersection decisions.

The report separated 24 MTR-smoothed choices, three map-generated supplements
and eight forced-direction probes without confirmed road targets. None of
these counts is a driving success rate. Of the 27 road-target routes, 26 kept
the target under all three steering assumptions; 21 retained risk flags.

Evidence: runs/project-v3/instruction-priority-v4/index.html and the preceding
language-choice review. The new repository's map-only track is described in
the current architecture; these earlier selector rules are not its runtime.
