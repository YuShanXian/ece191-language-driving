Historical research checkpoint

The original clip had 1,035 decoded video frames and GPS rows. The second clip
had 1,234 frames and 1,235 GPS rows; camera lookup found no image for its final
GPS row. This supports a conditional mapping, not independent release-wide
alignment certification. Two actual doPlan annotations were integrated into
viewing and observed-state replay.

The baseline used 18 windows across separate development and evaluation
recordings. Masking future state in three development inputs left the MTR
outputs unchanged. Additional tracking, traffic context and raw-image checks
were recorded separately. Local repeatability does not establish a fresh
installation, closed-loop traffic driving or a general language benchmark.
See the related archived notes for scope and limitations.
