How modeled tracking changed candidate availability

An optional filter reused saved tracking trials and required retention of the
target margin under all six settings. It did not retrain or rerun MTR. The
population contained 30 still-active time/direction combinations; completed
windows remained completed.

Available selections changed from 6/30 to 2/30. One development right-lane
choice changed from candidate 25 to 10; a second-annotation left-lane choice
retained candidate 14. Four other previous suggestions were rejected.
This is candidate availability under a development filter, not driving success.
Unexamined cases were not treated as passing. The selection toggle was distinct
from the display-only tracking overlay.

Evidence: config/tracking-selection-v1.json and
runs/project-v2/tracking-selection-v1/{evaluation,verification}.json.
