Current signal state and map controls

The audit queried exact current timestamps, retaining missing, unknown and
conflicting signal states. It did not use future signals or assume missing
means green. Existing occupancy of a connector was distinguished from a new
predicted entry; center-sample entry intervals were not front-edge crossing times.

Of 62 candidates passing the earlier baseline, 34 predicted a new connector
entry: three involved a current red signal and 31 had unresolved light state.
The remaining 28 had no new entry. Arrival-time permission was unknown, so this
was not a count of proven red-light violations. Stop-region associations and
turn-type fields were descriptive rather than legal rules.

The audit did not change candidate ranking or resolve the language phrase
"that light". Evidence: runs/project-v2/traffic-context-v1/ and
config/traffic-context-v1.json. Directional stop-line semantics and legal
interpretation remained future work.
