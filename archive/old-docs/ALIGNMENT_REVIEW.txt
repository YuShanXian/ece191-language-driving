Raw camera alignment review

The original packet fixed sample positions before comparison: clip endpoints,
annotation boundaries, intermediate points and model checkpoints. It contains
12 CAM_F0 filenames obtained from the database, with six samples per clip.
The second clip's last GPS row has no camera image; no extra frame was invented.

The later comparison obtained original images from official nuPlan camera
archives using bounded byte ranges and archive-entry checks. All 12 nearest
video frames matched the expected frame, with no ties. This is a spot check,
not certification of every frame or confirmation by the dataset publisher.
The annotation start remains different from a known passenger speaking time.

Evidence: runs/project-v2/alignment-review-v1/review-packet.json and
runs/project-v2/raw-image-comparison-v1/{comparison,verification,visual-review}.json.
The first packet's earlier missing-image status was retained historically.
