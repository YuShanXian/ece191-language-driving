Historical research priorities

The note distinguished completed timing, controller, terminal-hold and traffic
context diagnostics from proposed work. These diagnostics did not solve legal
crossings, missing signal state, traffic interaction or full maneuver coverage.

Further work required a directional stop-line definition, clear treatment of
unknown/current/future signal state, reliable lane-boundary meanings, and more
balanced turning scenarios. Candidate failures should be retained and explained.
Recorded futures may support offline diagnostics but must not leak into online
selection. Background language labels need timing evidence before becoming
instruction ground truth. These are proposals, not current capability claims or
instructions to restart historical experiments.
