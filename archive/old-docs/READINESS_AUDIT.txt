Historical readiness audit

The prototype supported local offline demonstrations and repeatable comparisons,
while the complete research objective remained unfinished. The audit separated
data alignment, multi-record inputs, language parsing, observed-stage tracking,
candidate selection and vehicle-model checks.

The fixed baseline used distinct development/evaluation records. Small original
camera spot checks supported a conditional mapping, not full independent
alignment certification. Controlled grammar coverage was not language accuracy.
Real-driver replay was not execution of selected candidates. Geometry and
modeled tracking provided limited counterexamples and evidence, not real-world
safety. New turning scenes exposed sparse candidate coverage and a partial
left turn that failed subsequent modeled road checks.

The audit retained negative results, unknown traffic conditions and missing
fresh-installation/closed-loop evidence. Historical passing test totals must
not be substituted for current repository verification or project completion.
