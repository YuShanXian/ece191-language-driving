Trajectory geometry and steering are different checks

MTR predicts future center positions. A path lying within a lane and satisfying
simple acceleration bounds does not establish that a vehicle can follow it.
The diagnostic reconstructed headings and examined steering-related behavior,
including sensitivity to the assumed body orientation and low-speed segments.

Some earlier accepted lane changes did not retain the target-body margin under
an alternative heading assumption. Subsequent reserve-tail experiments generated
paths with an explicit geometric margin, but that margin was not calibrated
against real map and vehicle errors. Tracking experiments were needed to test
whether the modeled vehicle actually retained the intended lane target.
See TRACKING_DIAGNOSTIC.txt and TRACKING_SELECTION.txt.
