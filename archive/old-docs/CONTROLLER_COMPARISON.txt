LQR and iLQR following the same saved routes

The comparison used nine lane-change paths and one partial left turn, two
controllers and three initial steering assumptions, totaling 60 runs. Both
used a 0.2-second control period and 0.05-second motion step. The common horizon
was 5.8 seconds because the installed iLQR initializer failed when too few
reference points remained. No future reference points were fabricated.

iLQR disabled the wall-clock timeout but retained the iteration limit and
convergence settings. It had lower maximum center error in 16 of 30 pairs and
higher error in 14. Road checks passed 27/30 for LQR and 30/30 for iLQR.
iLQR used the full iteration allowance in 867/870 solves; neither convergence
nor real-time capability was established. The comparison concerns complete
configurations, not the algorithm name alone. The selected left turn remained
partial. Evidence: runs/project-v2/controller-comparison-v1/ and
config/controller-comparison-v1.json.
